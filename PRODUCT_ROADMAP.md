# Nazo product roadmap

A quick-reading backlog for future Nazo work. It keeps the full 74-idea set in one place; it is a direction, not a promise or release schedule.

## Legend

- **★** — strongest recommendation
- **P0** — build first · **P1** — high value · **P2** — worthwhile · **P3** — explore later
- **S** — small · **M** — medium · **L** — large

## Recommended first batch

Build these in order because they form one clear journey from launch to learning:

- **#1 Quiz-launch portal** — connect local-ready starts on Home—including Daily Challenge and Practice Your Misses—to the first question without duplicating AI Loading. **Implemented.**
- **#2 Answer choreography** — make every answer feel immediate and clear. **Implemented.**
- **#3 Animated results story** — turn completion into a satisfying summary. **Implemented.**
- **#4 Instant mistake rematch** — convert errors into one-tap practice. **Implemented.**
- **#5 Topic mastery foundation** — make long-term learning visible. **Implemented.**
- **#6 Smart Daily ladder** — turn the Daily into a visible Easy-to-Hard path with a clear finish. **Implemented.**
- **#7 Adaptive difficulty** — tune upcoming questions from accuracy, pace, hints, recent misses, and topic mastery. **Implemented.**
- **#8 Quiz collections** — save and quickly restore favorite topic, mode, difficulty, and length combinations. **Implemented.**
- **#9 Boss question** — end selected finite runs with a higher-value question that feels like a finale. **Implemented.**
- **#10 Personalized Home focus** — surface the best next action from streaks, misses, and recent topics. **Implemented.**
- **#11 Streak protection token** — earn limited protection through play rather than notifications or payment. **Implemented.**
- **#12 Shareable result card** — export a polished result image without exposing profile or quiz content. **Implemented.**
- **#13 Friendly rival ghost** — race a saved local score-and-time profile without live multiplayer. **Implemented.**

## Game modes

29. **★ Mistake sprint** — **P1 · M** — A fast run made entirely from recent wrong answers.
30. **Sudden death** — **P2 · S** — One wrong answer ends the run; score is the correct streak.
31. **Confidence mode** — **P2 · M** — Answer, then rate confidence so lucky guesses can be separated from mastery.
32. **Category climb** — **P2 · L** — Clear themed tiers from broad knowledge to specialist questions.
33. **Reverse quiz** — **P2 · M** — Show an answer or clue and choose the matching question, character, or series.
34. **Timeline order** — **P3 · L** — Arrange events, arcs, releases, or transformations in the correct sequence.
35. **Connection chains** — **P3 · L** — Link characters, studios, powers, creators, or series through shared clues.
36. **Draft-and-duel Versus** — **P2 · L** — Players choose categories before alternating equal question sets.
37. **Co-op lives** — **P3 · L** — Two players share hearts and try to extend one local run.
38. **Weekly gauntlet** — **P2 · L** — A seeded multi-stage challenge that stays comparable for the whole week.
39. **Mystery topic** — **P2 · M** — Hide the topic until clues across several questions reveal it.
40. **Speed sorting** — **P3 · L** — Drag items into groups under a short timer with accessible tap controls too.
41. **Study mode** — **P1 · M** — Remove score pressure, allow reveal-first learning, and repeat weak material.

## Progression and retention

42. **★ Topic mastery map** — **P0 · L** — Build the durable topic-level model needed by practice and recommendations.
43. **Weekly learning recap** — **P1 · M** — Summarize play, improvement, weak topics, and one suggested next step.
44. **Meaningful level rewards** — **P1 · M** — Unlock visual choices, challenge variants, or titles rather than empty numbers.
45. **Badge progress previews** — **P1 · S** — Show exact progress toward locked achievements when it does not spoil them.
46. **Personal best timeline** — **P2 · M** — Record notable accuracy, speed, Survival, Blitz, and streak milestones.
47. **Topic comeback goals** — **P2 · M** — Suggest a short return session when confidence in a past topic has decayed.
48. **Flexible streak goals** — **P2 · M** — Let players choose daily, three-days-a-week, or weekend routines.
49. **Mastery crowns** — **P2 · M** — Award topic crowns from sustained evidence, with visible requirements and decay rules.
50. **Collection titles** — **P3 · M** — Earn profile titles from varied accomplishments, not repetitive grinding.
51. **Seasonal archive, not pass** — **P3 · L** — Add optional themed chapters that remain playable after the season ends.
52. **Gentle return path** — **P1 · S** — Welcome returning players with a recap and easy next action, never guilt language.

59. **Large-screen gameplay frame** — **P2 · M** — Constrain reading width while using side space for progress and mode information.
60. **Setup validation in place** — **P1 · S** — Explain unavailable topics/providers beside the relevant control before a launch fails.
61. **Searchable settings** — **P3 · M** — Find appearance, sound, provider, backup, and accessibility options by name.

## Logic and technical quality

62. **★ Unified launch coordinator** — **P1 · L** — Model local, cached, generated, retry, fallback, and cancellation as explicit launch states.
63. **Deterministic question identity** — **P1 · M** — Normalize IDs so cache, history, misses, and anti-repeat logic agree.
64. **Question quality scoring** — **P1 · L** — Reject ambiguous, duplicate, malformed, or giveaway AI questions before play.
65. **Cache freshness policy** — **P1 · M** — Balance instant launches with topic variety, age, model changes, and offline availability.
66. **Session event ledger** — **P2 · L** — Record idempotent gameplay events so restore, stats, and rewards cannot diverge.
67. **Timer state machine** — **P1 · M** — Share pause, resume, process-restoration, and deadline rules across timed modes.
68. **Seeded challenge reproducibility** — **P2 · M** — Make Daily and weekly sets reproducible for debugging and fair comparison.
69. **Offline capability map** — **P1 · S** — Let each mode declare what works locally instead of scattering provider checks.
70. **Performance and motion budget** — **P1 · M** — Track startup, frame time, image memory, battery, and reduced-motion compliance.

## Ideas to avoid for now

71. **Real-time online multiplayer** — **Avoid · L** — Servers, moderation, latency, accounts, and cheating would dominate the product.
72. **Open public user-question marketplace** — **Avoid · L** — Unreviewed content creates copyright, safety, spoiler, and quality risks.
73. **Loot boxes, energy, or pay-to-continue** — **Avoid · M** — These weaken trust and turn learning loops into pressure loops.
74. **Unskippable cinematic motion everywhere** — **Avoid · M** — Keep spectacle for meaningful moments and always respect reduced motion.

## Product guardrails

- Preserve offline play, local fallback, provider choice, retries, cancellation, and session recovery.
- Prefer clear feedback over decorative delay; motion must explain a state change.
- Keep one live app/navigation tree during transitions.
- Never make streaks, reminders, or progression shame the player.
- Test every new mode with reduced motion, large text, rotation, process restoration, and no network.
