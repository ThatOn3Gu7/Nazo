## New

- **Press and hold your profile picture to see it full size.** A quick tap still opens the "Change profile picture" pop-up exactly as before.
- **Choose how the sound effects sound.** Settings → Feedback → Sound style offers Chime, Arcade, Marimba and Bell. Tap one to hear it; the choice applies to every sound in the app. Chime is the original sound and stays the default.

## Improved

- **Quiz questions show which anime they are about.** Each question now carries its series name (for example, "Attack on Titan" on a "What is the name of the first arc?" question), so generic wording can no longer be mistaken for another anime's question.

## Fixed

- **Pop-ups no longer vanish when you rotate the screen.** Opening the Backup or Restore preview and turning the phone used to close it with no warning. The pop-up now stays open and changes shape with the orientation — a centred card in portrait, a slide-up panel in landscape — and back again when you rotate back. The same fix applies to the profile picture, username and link pop-ups, the Send feedback chooser, and the app icon, background effects, celebrations and sparkles panels.
- **The "Change profile picture" pop-up now fits in landscape.** Turned sideways there was not enough height for the avatar grid, so it was squashed down to a sliver and the controls bunched up against the buttons. The pop-up now scrolls as a whole in landscape, so every avatar and button keeps its proper size. Portrait is unchanged.
- **The daily streak now resets when you miss a day.** Skipping a day left the old streak number on screen until you next finished a quiz. An inactive day is now recognised as soon as anything looks at your stats, so Home, Profile, Statistics and the widget all agree. Your best streak is never affected.
- **The update panel and the profile picture preview also survive rotation now.** Turning the phone while checking for an update, reading release notes, or previewing and cropping a new profile picture no longer throws the panel away.
- **Wrong answers in the question bank were put right.** One question credited Frieren with Trigun's priest and several "which arc comes next" questions had the wrong arc; the practice deck also mixed up similar questions from different series.
- **AI-generated quizzes can no longer be scored on a wrong answer.** If the AI's reply is broken (an answer that does not match its options, fewer than four choices, repeated choices, or not enough valid questions), the app now rejects it, quietly retries once with your alternate model, and otherwise offers the local quiz — instead of silently marking the first choice as "correct".
