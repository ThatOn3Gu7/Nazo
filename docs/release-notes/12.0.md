## New

- **Back gestures now show where they will take you.** On Android 13 and newer with gesture navigation, swipe from either edge to reveal the real destination screen beneath the current one. Release to go back or slide home to cancel; buttons and older devices keep the usual instant back.
- **Profile pictures can now move.** Choose a GIF, animated WebP or short MP4 avatar and preview it before saving. Animated avatars stay muted, loop smoothly across Home and Profile, open in the full-size viewer, and travel with your backups.
- **Profiles now have custom cover backgrounds.** The redesigned profile header can use your own photo, one of 14 included artworks, or one of five animated gradients while keeping the avatar, toolbar and profile details easy to read.
- **Cover backgrounds can be videos or match your avatar automatically.** Pick a short MP4 for a muted looping cover, or let Nazo create a soft colour-rich image or blurred video background from the current avatar. Automatic covers continue following later avatar changes without replacing saved manual choices.

## Improved

- **Changing a cover feels smooth and intentional.** Photos, gradients, videos and automatic backgrounds crossfade with a restrained depth effect instead of snapping. Rapid taps settle on the latest choice, animated gradients keep moving, and loading media never fades in as a black rectangle.
- **Animated media stays seamless while you move around the app.** Avatar and cover videos keep independent playback, preserve their place through previews and navigation, and avoid creating extra video players just for visual transitions. Android's “Remove animations” setting is respected throughout.
