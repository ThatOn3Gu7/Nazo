## New

- **Back gestures show where they will take you.** Swipe from the screen edge and the current screen slides away with your finger, revealing the screen behind it — release to go back, or slide it back to change your mind. The preview needs gesture navigation on Android 13 or newer; buttons and older devices keep the usual instant back, and with Android's "Remove animations" setting the screen changes without the preview.
