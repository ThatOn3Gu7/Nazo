## New

- **Back gestures show where they will take you — Nazo's biggest new interaction.** Swipe from the screen edge and the current screen slides away with your finger, revealing exactly the screen you would land on, at full frame rate and in both directions. Release past halfway and Nazo glides into that screen; change your mind and slide back — the screen settles smoothly into place, without a jolt. The preview plays wherever a back would navigate — Settings and its sections, results, profiles and the rest — and it runs on Nazo's own navigation, so the screen you see behind the gesture is always the real destination. It needs gesture navigation on Android 13 or newer; buttons and older devices keep the usual instant back, and with Android's "Remove animations" accessibility setting the screen changes without the preview.
